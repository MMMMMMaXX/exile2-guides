# Community Questions — accuracy-distance-penalty-hit-chance

Real player confusions this article answers (paraphrased; no public source URLs were available, marked accordingly).

1. "Why do I miss when my tooltip shows ~100% hit chance?"
   - Explained via the distance penalty: the panel value is measured at a reference distance, not at long range.
   - Source: no public source (derived from brief P0).

2. "Do my spells need Accuracy?"
   - Clarified that most spells bypass the Accuracy check; over-investing Accuracy on spell builds is wasted.
   - Source: no public source.

3. "Do my minions use my Accuracy?"
   - Answered: minions follow their own accuracy rules, not the player's. Do not infer from PoE1.
   - Source: no public source (brief P0).

4. "Why do bosses dodge everything but trash mobs don't?"
   - Explained as high-Evasion bosses lowering hit chance well below map-clearing numbers.
   - Source: no public source.

5. "Does Resolute Technique make me always hit?"
   - Answered with the critical-strike trade-off caveat.
   - Source: no public source.
