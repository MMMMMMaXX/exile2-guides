# Formula Check — accuracy-distance-penalty-hit-chance

The brief (P0) forbids publishing a precise Chance-to-Hit formula or exact distance numbers that cannot be sourced from a current PoE2 reference.

Published structure (not a fixed coefficient formula):
- Hit chance is derived by comparing the attacker's Accuracy Rating to the target's Evasion Rating, with a diminishing-return curve.
- A separate distance factor reduces effective accuracy for ranged attacks as the target gets farther away; melee is unaffected.
- A missed hit cannot crit.

Coefficient status:
- The exact curve coefficients and the distance falloff start/rate are version-sensitive in PoE2 Early Access 0.5.4.
- These are NOT stated as numbers. The article directs readers to confirm them in the current client.
- No PoE1 formula was copied. This avoids the P0 "publish a precise formula you cannot source" error.
