# Terminology Map — accuracy-distance-penalty-hit-chance

Key terms and how the article defines them.

| Term | Definition in article |
|------|----------------------|
| Accuracy Rating | Offensive stat your attacks use to beat a target's Evasion. |
| Evasion Rating | Defensive stat that lets a target avoid incoming attacks. |
| Chance to Hit | Displayed percentage that an attack will connect. |
| Distance penalty | Reduction of effective accuracy for ranged attacks as range grows. |
| Always Hit / Cannot Be Evaded | Skill or effect tags that bypass or ignore the evasion check. |
| Resolute Technique | Keystone that removes Evasion as a factor, trading off crit scaling. |
| Hits Cannot Be Evaded | Effect that ignores target Evasion for your hits, with build trade-offs. |

EN and ZH files use identical term IDs; ZH provides the Simplified Chinese translation of each definition.
