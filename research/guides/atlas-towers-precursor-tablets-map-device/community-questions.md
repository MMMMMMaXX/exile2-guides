# Community Questions

Real player questions this article answers (paraphrased from community discussion).

1. "I can't put the tablet in the tower — it does nothing when I click it."
   Resolution: clear the Tower first (activates beacon), then place from Atlas screen, not inventory.
   Public source: bot-lobbies 0.5 guide (https://bot-lobbies.vercel.app/blog/poe2-tablets-and-towers-guide)

2. "My tablet is not working / the map doesn't show the mechanic."
   Resolution: check radius, remaining uses, and map-type applicability; branch, do not assume one cause.
   Public source: no single public source; resolved via diagnostic in article.

3. "Do tablets go in the Map Device?"
   Resolution: no — Map Device runs the Waystone; tablets go in the Tower via Atlas screen.
   Public source: poe2wiki Tablet page (https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Tablet)

4. "How many tablets per tower / how many charges?"
   Resolution: up to 3 slots; charges described qualitatively. Exact numbers pending in-game check.
   Public source: bot-lobbies (states 10 charges); flagged Medium confidence.

5. "Can I upgrade a tablet with a Regal Orb?"
   Resolution: Augmentation can add a mod to Magic tablets; Regal Orb does not normally make a Rare tablet.
   Public source: poe2wiki Tablet page (rarity notes).
