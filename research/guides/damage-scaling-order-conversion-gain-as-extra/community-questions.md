3. **"What does 'Gain as Extra' do?"** — Adds a new damage type on top without removing the original. (See card: Gain as Extra keeps the base.)
4. **"Is 'Damage Taken' my own damage buff?"** — No, it only affects incoming damage on the target. (See card: Damage Taken is not your damage.)
5. **"Why does my planner differ from my tooltip?"** — Different assumptions about buffs, enemy resists, accuracy, distance. (See common-mistakes section.)
6. **"Where does conversion sit relative to increased and more?"** — After increased/more, before enemy resistance. (See order table + FAQ.)
