# Version Diff — damage-scaling-order-conversion-gain-as-extra

## PoE1 vs PoE2 (avoid copying PoE1)
- **PoE1 conversion chain:** a strict ordered chain (Phys → Lightning → Cold → Fire → Chaos) where earlier conversions feed later ones; 100% chains common.
- **PoE2 current:** conversion is a distinct pipeline step and the strict PoE1 chain ordering is not assumed here. The article describes conversion qualitatively and flags the exact ordering as version-sensitive. Do NOT teach the PoE1 chain as if it applies to PoE2.
- **Leech / charges / other mechanics:** out of scope; this article focuses on damage scaling order only.

## Baseline
- Patch string kept at baseline "Path of Exile 2 Early Access 0.5.4" per spec.
- Any future 0.5.4d change to conversion ordering would be noted here, but none was confirmed for this article.
