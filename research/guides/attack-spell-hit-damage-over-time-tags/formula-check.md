# Formula Check

This article is a definitions/mechanics guide, not a numeric formula-breakdown. No exact coefficients are invented.

## Structure stated (correct shape, no fabricated numbers)

- Damage of a Hit scales with Increased bonuses of the same category adding together, separate Increased categories and all More multipliers multiplying. The article describes this conceptually where relevant (e.g. generic vs category-specific modifiers) without inventing coefficients.
- DoT magnitude scales with Damage-over-Time multipliers and ailment magnitude; resistances in the matching type still mitigate. Penetration and armour do NOT apply because they are hit-only. This structure is sourced from the PoE2 wiki DoT page.

## Version-sensitive items (flagged pending PC verification)

- Whether Damage over Time / ailments can leech in 0.5.4 (Recovery changes in the 0.5 line). Stated qualitatively only.
- Exact modifier names and whether Minion/Totem-owned skills accept specific supports (flagged version-sensitive, not asserted as universal).

No numeric coefficient is presented as fact where it could not be sourced.
