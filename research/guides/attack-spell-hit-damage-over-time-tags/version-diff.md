# Version Diff

What changed vs older guides / legacy PoE1 info to avoid.

## PoE1 vs PoE2 differences (do NOT copy PoE1 rules)

- In PoE2, Spells are explicitly not affected by Accuracy or evasion, and their base damage/crit come from the skill. This is consistent with PoE1 but the article is written from the PoE2 wiki wording.
- Charges no longer auto-grant stats in PoE2 (out of scope here, but flagged so readers don't assume charge-based attack/spell interactions behave like PoE1).
- Recovery (Leech) behaviour changed in the 0.5 line; whether DoT can leech is therefore flagged as version-sensitive for 0.5.4 and marked pending PC verification.

## Legacy misunderstandings to avoid

- "Attack = Physical, Spell = Elemental" is false in PoE2. Attacks can be Fire; Spells can be Physical. The article states the categories are orthogonal.
- "DoT can crit/leech like a hit" is false. DoT is not a Hit in PoE2 (per current wiki).
- "Penetration helps DoT" is false; penetration only applies to Hits.

Patch string kept at baseline "Path of Exile 2 Early Access 0.5.4"; no 0.5.4d change central to this mechanic was confirmed, so patch string unchanged.
