# Terminology Map

Key terms and how the article defines them.

| Term | Definition in article |
|------|----------------------|
| Attack | A skill that directly uses your equipped weapon to damage enemies; rolls Accuracy vs evasion; can deal any damage type. |
| Spell | A skill using raw magic; not an Attack; ignores Accuracy/evasion; base damage & crit from the skill. |
| Hit | A single discrete instance of damage at a moment; can crit, leech, trigger on-hit. |
| Damage over Time (DoT) | Damage applied gradually across a duration; not a Hit; cannot crit/leech/trigger on-hit the same way. |
| Ailment | An abnormal status that deals DoT (Ignite, Bleed, Poison); not a Hit. |
| Skill Type | Attack vs Spell — the skill's category. |
| Damage Type | Physical, Fire, Cold, Lightning, Chaos — orthogonal to Skill Type. |
| Behaviour Tag | Projectile, AoE, Melee, Duration, Minion, Totem — describes delivery/ownership, not damage type. |

The core teaching point: Skill Type, Damage Type, Hit Type, and Behaviour Tags are four independent layers.
