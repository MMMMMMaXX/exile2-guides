# Formula Check — power-frenzy-endurance-charges

This article is mechanic-focused rather than a single numeric formula-breakdown, but the structural rules worth recording:

## Structural formula for charge value
- A charge has value ONLY when consumed: `effect = base_effect + consume_bonus(charge_type, count_consumed)`.
- The article does NOT assign numeric `consume_bonus` coefficients because they are version-sensitive (per-event damage/projectile counts differ by skill and patch).

## Independent dials (must not be multiplied incorrectly)
- Maximum, Duration, and generation amount are independent.
- Per the mechanics cluster rule: Increased bonuses of the same category add; separate Increased categories and all More multipliers multiply. The article describes consumption additively/multiplicatively only qualitatively (e.g. "extra projectiles and more damage per charge") without inventing coefficients.

## Version-sensitive items flagged
- Combat Frenzy cooldown (stated qualitatively as "short cooldown").
- Exact per-skill consume bonuses (omitted; reader told to confirm in-game).
- Default per-type maximum of 3 (stated; Endurance confirmed by Game Rant, others by pattern).

No coefficients were fabricated.
