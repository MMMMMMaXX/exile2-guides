# Version Diff — power-frenzy-endurance-charges

## PoE1 vs PoE2 (the key change to avoid copying)

- PoE1: charges granted passive stat bonuses while held (Power = crit, Frenzy = attack/move speed, Endurance = phys reduction/resist). Stacking and maintaining them was the goal.
- PoE2 (0.5.4): charges grant NO passive bonus by possession. They are resources consumed by skills/passives that explicitly interact with them. Value comes from the consuming effect.

## What the article reflects (current PoE2)

- Charges as generate-then-spend loops.
- Resonance keystone for charge-type conversion (real, sourced).
- Combat Frenzy generation on Freeze/Electrocute/Pin (current; cooldown value version-sensitive).
- Endurance generation/consumption list per Game Rant (Magma Barrier, Break Endurance, Cranial Impact, Grit, Infernal Cry, etc.).
