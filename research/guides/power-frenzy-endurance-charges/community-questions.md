# Community Questions — power-frenzy-endurance-charges

Real player confusions this article answers (paraphrased; see source-ledger for citations):

1. "Do I get free crit/attack speed just by holding charges like in PoE1?"
   -> No. In PoE2 charges are spent resources; only a consuming skill gives value.

2. "I raised my Maximum Charges but feel no stronger — why?"
   -> Maximum, Duration, and generation amount are separate numbers.

3. "Why does my charge plan fall apart on bosses?"
   -> Mapping uptime (kills/status effects) does not transfer to bosses that resist those triggers.

4. "Can I feed my Frenzy consumer with Power Charges?"
   -> No. The three types are distinct; only Resonance or a type-specific source changes what you gain.

5. "How do I actually make Frenzy Charges?"
   -> Combat Frenzy (Freeze/Electrocute/Pin), Cull the Weak, Sniper's Mark crit, Retort, Frenzy-on-Kill.

6. "What is the Resonance keystone for?"
   -> Redirects which charge type you generate, funneling into the charge your consumers want.
