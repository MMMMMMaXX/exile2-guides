# Community Questions — omen-crafting-prefix-suffix-control

Real player questions this article answers (paraphrased from common crafting-discussion themes; cited where a real URL exists, else marked "no public source").

1. **"My Omen didn't trigger — why?"**
   Mismatched currency or unmet item precondition. Answered in `community-evidence` and `troubleshooting`.
   - Source: no public source (derived from Mobalytics activation notes).

2. **"Which Omen should I use first?"**
   Match the currency you'll use and the side you want to affect. Answered in `community-evidence`.
   - Source: Mobalytics Omen Crafting (https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/omen-crafting).

3. **"Can I stack Omens?"**
   Yes, multiple can be active; combination is per-Omen. Answered in `omen-stack-planner` and `community-evidence`.
   - Source: Mobalytics Omen Crafting.

4. **"Sinistral vs Dextral — what's the difference?"**
   Sinistral = prefix, Dextral = suffix. Answered in FAQ.
   - Source: poe2wiki Omens (https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Omens).

5. **"Are Omens consumed if I never use the currency?"**
   No — only consumed when the matching action happens. Answered in FAQ.
   - Source: Mobalytics Omen Crafting.

6. **"Where do I get Omens?"**
   Ritual (Tribute) and Alva's Currency Exchange. Answered in FAQ.
   - Source: Mobalytics / IGN Omens.
