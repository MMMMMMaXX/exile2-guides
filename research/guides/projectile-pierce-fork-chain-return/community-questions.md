# Community Questions — projectile-pierce-fork-chain-return

1. "Why does Chain feel weak on a boss?" — Chain needs another enemy to redirect to; on a lone target it does little. Source: Last Protagonist video (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t-GpBSwf6kE).
2. "Can my skill both Pierce and Fork on the same hit?" — No; only one behaviour per collision. No public source needed; answered from wiki priority.
3. "Does Return hit the same enemy again?" — Yes, one extra collision per set on already-hit targets. Source: poe2wiki Return.
4. "Why won't my projectile chain off terrain?" — Skill may be forbidden (Lightning Arrow) or it is chaining from an enemy it can also pierce/fork. Source: poe2wiki Chain.
5. "Is Fork the same as Split or just more projectiles?" — No; Fork = two at ±60°, Split = auto-targeting copies, Projectile Count = separate projectiles. No public source.
6. "My Chain support isn't triggering" — likely enemy-chain skipped because Pierce/Fork present, no valid target in range, or skill cannot chain. No public source.
