# Unlock Route — atlas-masters-jado-hilda-doryani

## Jado

1. Sealed Vault (upper Ziggurat Refuge, mountain by Fortress wall) -> meet Jado.
2. Recover artifacts: Jade Isles, Sacred Reservoir, Derelict Mansion.
3. Each step unlocks next row + 1 point.

## Doryani

1. Get Rare Waystone, roll to 4 explicit modifiers.
2. Open -> Corrupted Nexus spawns -> clear it.
3. Row 1 + 1 point; further Nexus completions open later rows.

## Hilda

1. Campsite before Withered Willow (left of Ziggurat Refuge).
2. She marks a Great Beast; complete map + kill boss.
3. Repeat for 4 Great Beasts -> all 4 rows.

NOTE: exact row-gating per quest step should be re-confirmed on client.
