# Community Questions

Real player questions this guide answers (paraphrased from common PoE 2 discussions).

1. **"I equipped a Charm but I still got frozen / bled / ignited."**
   Cause is usually empty charges, wrong Charm for the ailment, unsocketed Belt, or content that disables Charms. Covered in `community-evidence` and `troubleshooting`. URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PwjzRjqCFKk

2. **"Is 75% fire resistance the same as Ignite immunity?"**
   No — resistance lowers damage but the ailment still applies. Only immunity prevents it. Covered in `avoiding-and-mitigating` and `faq`.

3. **"How do Charm charges refill?"**
   At Wells or by killing monsters. Covered in `charm-basics` and `faq`.

4. **"Can I apply Bleed / Poison to every boss?"**
   Not reliably — bosses have high thresholds. Covered in `boss-and-player-differences` and `faq`.

5. **"How many Charms can I equip?"**
   Limited by Belt sockets; exact count version-dependent. Covered in `charm-basics` and `faq`.

6. **"Why did my Charm not trigger in time?"**
   Entered fight with empty charges. Covered in `community-evidence`.

No public URL for items 2–6 beyond the wiki and video above; marked "no separate public source" where applicable.
