# Conflict Log

Conflicting community info encountered and how it was resolved.

1. **Charm charge costs** — wiki `List of charms` lists a specific charge cost per base type, but community clips quote different numbers from older patches. _Resolution:_ cite the wiki as the current source and flag exact costs `pending-pc`; do not invent alternate numbers.
2. **Boss ailment immunity** — some forum posts claim certain bosses are "fully immune" to Bleed/Poison. _Resolution:_ the article avoids absolute immunity claims and states bosses have high thresholds (possible-but-difficult), matching the wiki's threshold framing.
3. **Resistance vs immunity confusion** — recurring player belief that capping resistance prevents the ailment. _Resolution:_ `avoiding-and-mitigating` and `faq` state explicitly that Resistance ≠ Immunity.
4. **Charm socket count** — exact number of Belt sockets varies by Belt. _Resolution:_ described qualitatively; readers directed to the in-game Belt tooltip.
