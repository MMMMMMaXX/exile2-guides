# Version Diff

What changed vs older / legacy PoE 2 ailment & charm info to avoid repeating.

- **Player base Ailment Threshold** changed across patches (e.g. 0.2.0 made it half of Max Life). We describe it qualitatively as "a fraction of Max Life" and flag exact values `pending-pc` rather than citing a stale number.
- **Charm charge economy** was rebalanced in 0.2.0f (max charges and charges-per-use roughly halved for most Charms except Golden). We avoid asserting pre-0.5.4 charge numbers; the article references current wiki values and marks pending-pc.
- **Avoid claiming boss immunity/controllability** unless a current source confirms — older guides sometimes overstated this. Our `boss-and-player-differences` section treats it as possible-but-difficult.
- Target client: 0.5.4. Any numeric claim from earlier patches is treated as potentially outdated and not asserted as fact.
