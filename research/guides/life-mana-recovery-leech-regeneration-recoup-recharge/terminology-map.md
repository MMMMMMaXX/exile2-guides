# Terminology Map — life-mana-recovery-leech-regeneration-recoup-recharge

| Term | Definition used in article |
|------|----------------------------|
| Leech | Recovery of a % of damage you deal as a resource, only for the Damage Type/Hit type named by the modifier. |
| Regeneration | Continuous, always-on recovery over time; not tied to hitting or being hit; not stopped at full. |
| Recoup | Delayed recovery of a % of damage taken from hits, over ~8s; separate from regen; runs even at full. |
| Recharge | ES-only refill after a no-damage delay; interrupted by any damaging hit. |
| Flask | Instant on-use Life/Mana burst, limited by charges from kills/time. |
| Damage Type / Hit type | The specific category (e.g. physical, attack) a leech modifier requires to trigger. |
| 40,000 cap | 0.5 Leech per-hit damage limit for leech calculation. |
