# Conflict Log — essence-crafting-guaranteed-modifiers

Conflicting community info encountered and how it was resolved.

1. **Multi-essence stacking vs one-essence rule.**
   - Conflict: pre-0.5.0 guides and some forum posts show multiple essence mods on one item; current patch allows only one.
   - Resolution: Treat the one-essence rule as valid (sinceVersion 0.5.0) and flag older guides as `outdated` in the `version-conflicts` section. Primary source: timesaver.gg 0.5.4 guide + wiki.

2. **Corrupted essence drop rate.**
   - Conflict: one community source cited an approximate per-Vaal chance; another said it is simply conditional.
   - Resolution: Did NOT state a precise percentage. Described the Vaal path as conditional ("may become a corrupted essence"), not guaranteed, per the brief.

3. **Allowed item bases.**
   - Conflict: lists of allowed bases vary by patch and by essence type.
   - Resolution: Avoided enumerating specific bases; instructed readers to check the in-game essence tooltip. Version-dependent detail flagged pending PC verification.

4. **"Fractured items give two guaranteed mods with essence."**
   - Conflict: a third-party site claimed fractured + essence yields two guaranteed mods.
   - Resolution: Not reproduced. Out of scope for this guide; would require PC verification. Omitted rather than asserted.
