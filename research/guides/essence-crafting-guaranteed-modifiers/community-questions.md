# Community Questions — essence-crafting-guaranteed-modifiers

Real player questions this guide answers (paraphrased from community discussion; sources cited where available).

1. **"Why can't I add a 2nd essence mod?"** — Players expect pre-0.5 stacking. Answered: 0.5 one-essence limit. Source: timesaver.gg (`https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-essence-guide`).
2. **"What does Perfect remove?"** — Confusion about Perfect behaviour. Answered: removes a mod, adds a special exclusive one. Source: timesaver.gg.
3. **"How do I get corrupted essences (Horror/Hysteria/Delirium/Insanity)?"** — Answered: Vaal an imprisoned Greater-essence monster before releasing (conditional). Source: timesaver.gg.
4. **"Do essences work on any item?"** — Answered: depends on rarity and class; check in-game tooltip. Source: wiki (`https://www.poe2wiki.net/`).
5. **"Can I still use other currency after an essence?"** — Answered: yes, within affix-slot rules. Source: timesaver.gg.
6. **"Where do essences appear in the endgame?"** — Answered: campaign through Atlas, boosted by Atlas passives/tablets. Source: no public source beyond wiki/general — marked qualitative.
