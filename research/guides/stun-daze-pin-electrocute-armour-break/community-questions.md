# Community Questions — stun-daze-pin-electrocute-armour-break

Real player questions this guide answers (paraphrased from community discussion; cited where a public source exists):

1. **"My stun meter is full but the boss doesn't get stunned — why?"**
   That is the light-stun meter; the Heavy Stun Meter is separate and bosses resist light stun.
   Source: Mobalytics Stun — http://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/stun

2. **"My support gem says it can't Stun — what does that mean?"**
   Some supports (e.g. Pin I) redirect Stun Buildup to Pin and disable Stun on the skill.
   Source: fextralife Pin I — http://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Pin

3. **"Is Daze worth using?"**
   Daze boosts Stun Buildup (~+50%, ~8s) and helps reach Heavy Stun faster; value grows in long fights.
   Source: GameRant Daze — https://gamerant.com/path-of-exile-2-daze-explained

4. **"Does Armour Break count as a stun/immobilise?"**
   No — it only raises damage taken.
   Source: no public source required; clarified in-article from poe2wiki armour framing.

5. **"Is Electrocute just Freeze with lightning?"**
   Not identical: Electrocute stops both movement and action; Freeze stops actions. Both feed Immobilise.
   Source: Mobalytics Immobilise — https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/immobilise

6. **"How do I actually lock down a boss?"**
   Build Daze + physical buildup to fill the Heavy Stun Meter, then burst during the short window.
   Source: Inner Blaze video — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pP7B63v2-I4
