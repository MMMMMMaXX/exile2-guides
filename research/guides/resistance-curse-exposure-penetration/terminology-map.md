# Terminology Map — resistance-curse-exposure-penetration

| Term | Article definition |
|------|--------------------|
| Resistance | Enemy's percentage damage reduction per damage type; player default cap 75%. |
| Resistance Reduction | Any effect lowering enemy resistance; commonly via Exposure and Curses. |
| Exposure | Debuff on the enemy reducing its resistance to the relevant element(s) by a % for a duration. |
| Curse | Debuff applied to enemy (e.g. Elemental Weakness) reducing resistance; limited by Curse Limit; bosses may have reduced Curse Effect. |
| Penetration | Property of your hit reducing the resistance value used for that hit; local, last step, typically cannot go below 0%; does NOT change persistent resistance. |
| Ignore Resistance | Rare effect treating the relevant resistance as 0% for that instance, bypassing further modification. |
| Inversion (context) | Effect like Rakiata's Flow that flips the resistance value; applied before penetration; lineage-limited. |

The core distinction taught: Curse/Exposure change the enemy (persistent while active); Penetration changes only your hit.
