# Community Questions — resistance-curse-exposure-penetration

Real player confusions this article answers (paraphrased; sources noted where available):

1. **"I stacked tons of penetration but my damage didn't change."** — Addressed in troubleshooting and diagnostic: enemy resistance already near 0% after reduction, or main skill uses inversion where penetration is irrelevant. (No public source; common in-game confusion.)

2. **"Does penetration work on Damage over Time?"** — Answer: generally no for standalone DoT; Exposure/Curses do help. Version-sensitive; marked pending PC verification. (No public source; common confusion.)

3. **"Why does my curse feel weak on bosses?"** — Bosses have reduced Curse Effect; the debuff is applied but weaker. (Forum evidence: th.pathofexile.com build threads mention boss curse reduction.)

4. **"Negative vs zero resistance — can Exposure/Curse push below 0%?"** — Yes, reduction can push actual value below zero; penetration is capped at 0% for the used value. (Elyxir explains reduction can go below zero.)

5. **"Should I use Exposure/Curses with Rakiata's Flow?"** — Usually no for the inverted main skill; reduction shrinks inversion payoff. (Elyxir 200.)

6. **"Are penetration glove implicits bad with inversion?"** — No; penetration is local and doesn't reduce the inverted value. (Elyxir 200.)
