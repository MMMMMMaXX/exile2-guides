# Version Diff

What changed versus older / legacy PoE2 defence info, to avoid stale claims.

- **Runic Ward is new in 0.5.** Pre-0.5 guides and the legacy 0.3/0.4 defence meta omit it entirely. Articles written before 0.5 should not be cited for Runic Ward behaviour.
- **Deflect rework (0.5).** Multiple current community guides note Deflect now scales from Evasion (≈17% conversion, 95% cap). This guide mentions Deflect only qualitatively as a layered avoidance system and does not assert exact conversion numbers.
- **Energy Shield recharge.** Documented wiki value (12.5%/sec after 4s delay) is treated as pending-pc against the live 0.5.4 client rather than asserted as current.
- **Resistance penalty.** The campaign -10%/act resistance penalty and 75% displayed cap nuance are out of scope for this layer-focused guide; left for the cross-linked resistances content.

Legacy info to avoid: any pre-0.5 "three-layer only" framing that excludes Runic Ward.
