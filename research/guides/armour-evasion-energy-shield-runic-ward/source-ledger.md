# Source Ledger

Real sources used to author the Armour / Evasion / Energy Shield / Runic Ward guide.

## Official (wiki)
- **PoE2 wiki — Armour** — `https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Armour` — confirms Armour is a physical-only, hit-size-scaling layer (not a flat %).
- **PoE2 wiki — Evasion** — `https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Evasion` — confirms Evasion is a chance to avoid hits; ailments are avoided on evaded hits.
- **PoE2 wiki — Energy Shield** — `https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Energy_Shield` — confirms ES absorbs before Life, recharges after a delay, chaos doubled, bleed/poison bypass.
- **PoE2 wiki — Runic Ward** — `https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Runic_Ward` — confirms Runic Ward is a separate 0.5 layer engaging at 1 Life.
