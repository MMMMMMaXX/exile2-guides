# Alternatives — Ghostwrithe

## vs Rare Vaal Regalia

- A rare ES chest can reach a higher base Energy Shield plus useful suffixes (ES%, reduced ES start delay, attribute req).
- It keeps life sustain (flask/leech/regen) intact.
- **Use the rare chest when** you want a life buffer or rely on life flasks.

## vs Chaos Inoculation keystone

- CI sets Maximum Life to 1 and you still build ES; life leech/regen on a separate bar still function.
- Ghostwrithe sets life to 0 and folds life passives into ES; life leech/regen are disabled.
- **Ghostwrithe wins** when your tree is heavy on life passives you want to keep paying off as ES, and you accept the sustain lockout.

## vs normal life armour

- Strictly worse for any flask/leech build. Those should never consider it.

## Decision rule

- Commit fully to one ES pool -> Ghostwrithe.
- Keep life + ES separate -> rare chest or CI.
