# Community Questions — Ghostwrithe

Three real-style community questions with direct, actionable answers (mirrored in the article's `community-evidence` section).

## Q1 — Life flask and leech dead?
**Question:** If 100% of my life is from Energy Shield, do my life flasks and life leech just stop working?
**Answer:** Yes. Once Maximum Life is folded into Energy Shield, life = 0, so life flasks, life leech and life regen have nothing to act on. Rebuild sustain on ES: recharge rate, ES leech (Ghostshroud), ES-on-block.

## Q2 — Free Chaos Inoculation?
**Question:** Is Ghostwrithe basically a free Chaos Inoculation without the keystone?
**Answer:** CI-adjacent. With 0 life, chaos cannot kill via life, so you get the chaos-safe feel without setting life to 1. Difference: your life passives now feed ES, but life leech/regen are disabled. Over-cap ES and add chaos resist — all damage hits one bar.

## Q3 — Buy unique or rare chest?
**Question:** Should I buy Ghostwrithe or a rare Vaal Regalia with higher base ES?
**Answer:** Only for an all-in ES build. Otherwise buy the rare chest with the highest base ES% and keep your life flask. Shop the highest base-ES roll either way; ignore the (20-30)% suffix roll.
