# Current vs Legacy — Ghostwrithe

## Current (0.5.4)

Ghostwrithe is a unique Vaal Regalia with three modifiers:

1. 100% of Maximum Life is from Energy Shield
2. (20-30)% of Hit Damage is taken from Energy Shield before Life
3. Cannot Leech or Regenerate Life, and Cannot gain Life from Flasks

This is the only current version in Early Access 0.5.4.

## Legacy / earlier EA

- Introduced in Path of Exile 2 Early Access around 0.2.0 as a conversion-body unique.
- No separate "legacy" value set is documented; the three modifiers have been stable since introduction.
- The (20-30)% tier is a fixed range and is not re-rollable by a Divine Orb.

## What changed across patches

- 0.5.0 (Runes of Aldur): low-level uniques became Runeforgeable. Ghostwrithe (level 1) qualifies — modifiers retained, base gains higher ES.
- No documented nerf or rework of the conversion mechanic through 0.5.x.
