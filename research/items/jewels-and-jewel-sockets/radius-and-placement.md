# Radius and Placement — Jewels and Jewel Sockets

## Radius tiers

- Basic jewels: none.
- Time-Lost (Ruby/Emerald/Sapphire): Medium-Large.
- Against the Darkness: Medium (current), was Large pre-0.2.0.

## Placement rules

- Preview the circle in the passive tree planner before buying.
- Count allocated Small and Notable nodes inside the circle.
- Same modifier scores differently per socket because coverage differs.
- Avoid far detours (4+ points) unless the roll is exceptional.
