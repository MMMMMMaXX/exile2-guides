# Item Family and Variants — Jewels and Jewel Sockets

Members covered: Basic Jewel, Time-Lost Jewel, Diamond Unique, Timeless Jewel, Sinister Jewel, Corrupted Jewel.

## Notes
- Basic jewels: direct mods, no radius, colored base gates affix pool.
- Time-Lost: Sekhemas radius jewels (Ruby/Emerald/Sapphire) + unique Against the Darkness on Diamond.
- Diamond Uniques: Against the Darkness, From Nothing, Controlled Metamorphosis, The Adorned, Megalomaniac — different mechanics.
- Timeless: Expedition Seed/Keystone conquest (Heroic Tragedy, Undying Hate).
- Sinister: gear sockets via Voices; reject Unique jewels.
- Corrupted: extra implicit, no further crafting.
