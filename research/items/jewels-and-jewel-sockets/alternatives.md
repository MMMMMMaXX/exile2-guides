# Alternatives — Jewels and Jewel Sockets

- From Nothing: remote Keystone allocation.
- Controlled Metamorphosis: ring-shaped remote allocation.
- The Adorned: amplifies corrupted Magic jewels.
- Megalomaniac: allocates Random Notables.
- Basic Time-Lost / Rare jewels: cheaper radius or direct bonuses.

Pick the alternative that matches the build's actual need (remote allocation vs radius bonus vs magic-jewel amp).
