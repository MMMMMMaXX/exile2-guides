# Alternatives - Ingenuity (机巧)

- **Rare heavy belt**: better when you already meet requirements / use few charms; brings life, resists, attributes and an open suffix.
- **Other unique belts**: stronger when you want a specific mechanic rather than a flat floor / charm multiplier.
- **No direct competitor**: Ingenuity is the only belt that scales equipped charms.

Decision: charms + Ingenuity cheaper early; mirror rare belt can exceed it late.
