# Build Usage - Ingenuity (机巧)

- **Charm-stacking defence build**: fills belt with resist/regen/phasing charms; 40% effect multiplies all.
- **Hardcore / league-start survivor**: safe leveling belt, multiply charms as you out-level them.
- **Group/support with area charms**: radius roll widens ally buffs.
- **Charge-oriented nature build**: effect roll amplifies charge-gain/duration buffs.

Hard prerequisite: a real charm plan (enough sockets, charms you trigger often). Without it, a rare belt is stronger.
