# Community Questions - Ingenuity (机巧)

1. **Q: How many charms before Ingenuity is worth the belt slot?**
   A: Roughly more than one charm you trigger often. With 2-3 charms a 40% effect roll beats a rare belt. One charm rarely justifies it.

2. **Q: Does the radius roll matter for solo play?**
   A: For self-only charms radius does nothing. Solo: ignore radius, want 40% effect. Group with area charms: value both.

3. **Q: What roll to buy; can Vaal improve effect?**
   A: Effect is a fixed 30-40% band; 40% is the ceiling. Vaal adds an implicit, not a higher effect number.

4. **Q: Do catalysts change what Ingenuity scales?**
   A: Catalysts shape a charm own magnitude; Ingenuity then multiplies it. They stack cleanly.
