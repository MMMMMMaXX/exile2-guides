# Item Family and Variants — From Nothing

Article ID: from-nothing

## Diamond Unique Jewel family
From Nothing is one of five build-enabling Diamond Unique Jewels. Members covered in the family-overview section:

| Member | Mechanism | Relationship to From Nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Against the Darkness | Two random radius modifiers boost small passives | Opposite problem: radius attribute, not Keystone |
| From Nothing | Remote allocation around a bound Keystone | This article |
| Controlled Metamorphosis | Ring-band remote allocation, Elemental Resistance cost | Cousin; ring shape vs disc |
| The Adorned | Amplifies corrupted Magic Jewels | Different niche (jewel scaling) |
| Megalomaniac | Grants 1-3 random Notables | Direct Notable allocation |

## Variants of From Nothing itself
From Nothing has no sub-variants; every copy differs only by the bound Keystone (30+ pool) and the fixed small radius. The only meaningful "variant" axis is the Keystone, which drives all trade value.
