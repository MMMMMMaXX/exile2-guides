## Current mechanic (0.5.4)
- Unique Diamond Jewel, Limited to 1, Requires Level 20.
- Binds to one specific Keystone on drop; forms a fixed small radius around that Keystone wherever it sits on the tree.
- Line: "Passives in Radius of [Keystone] can be Allocated without being connected to your tree."
- The Keystone itself is NOT allocated by the jewel; only surrounding small/Notable passives are remotely allocatable.
- You still pay the passive point for every allocated node; the jewel only removes pathing cost.
- Always Corrupted: cannot be chanced, divined, or otherwise currency-modified.
- Drops from the King in the Mists at the Crux of Nothingness at Difficulty 4 (+4 Crux of Nothingness Ritual Atlas). Requires "An Audience with the King".
