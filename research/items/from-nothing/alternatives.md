# Alternatives — From Nothing

Article ID: from-nothing

## Direct alternatives

- **Rare Diamond Jewel**: flat stats (life, attributes, resistances, damage) for the same socket, no Keystone dependency, no pathing trick. Usually better when target nodes are cheap to path to.
- **Controlled Metamorphosis**: ring-band remote allocation with an Elemental Resistance cost.
- **Megalomaniac**: direct random Notable allocation.
- **Against the Darkness**: boosts small passives in a radius.
- **Normal pathing**: often cheaper than a wrong-Keystone copy.

## When NOT to use

- Pathing to the target nodes is already short.
- The copy's bound Keystone does not match your build.
- You need the socket for a higher-value Rare/Unique jewel.
