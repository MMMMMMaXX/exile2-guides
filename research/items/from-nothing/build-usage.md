# Build Usage — From Nothing

Article ID: from-nothing

## Decision framework

Use From Nothing when ALL of the following hold:

- Your build wants passives near a specific Keystone.
- The bound Keystone of the copy you can acquire matches that need.
- The target nodes are expensive to path to normally (high net save).
- You can spare one Jewel Socket.

## Build personas (from article)

1. Off-class / hybrid enabling — reach opposite-side clusters.
2. Attribute-stacking setups — grab attribute nodes near a Keystone.
3. Distant Notable rush — take a high-value Notable day one.
4. Build-enabling Keystone access — reach a Keystone's neighbours (e.g. Chaos Inoculation area) without the full branch.

## Build decision example

A spellcaster wanting the Chaos Inoculation area nodes but not the full ES path can socket From Nothing bound to a nearby Keystone and grab those neighbours directly, saving 5-7 pathing points versus normal pathing.
