# Radius and Placement — From Nothing

Article ID: from-nothing

## Radius facts

- Shape: fixed **small disc** (filled circle), centered on the bound Keystone.
- The radius is fixed at drop; it cannot be rerolled (jewel is always Corrupted).
- Only nodes inside the disc are reachable. The Keystone at the center is explicitly excluded.

## Placement strategy

- Place the Jewel Socket somewhere that gives you a short path to the socket itself.
- The useful nodes are determined by where the bound Keystone sits — you cannot move the radius, only choose whether this copy fits your tree.
- Because the radius is small, From Nothing shines when the bound Keystone is near other nodes you want (off-class/hybrid, attribute stacking, distant Notable rush).

## Comparison to Controlled Metamorphosis

- From Nothing: filled disc, no resistance cost, Keystone-locked.
- Controlled Metamorphosis: annulus (ring band), Elemental Resistance cost, ring-size 1-8.
