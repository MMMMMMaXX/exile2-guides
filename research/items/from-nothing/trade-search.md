# Trade Search — From Nothing

Article ID: from-nothing

## Official trade site workflow

1. Search "From Nothing Diamond" in the main bar.
2. Click "Add Stat Filter" and type the Keystone name (e.g. Conduit, Resonance, Mind Over Matter).
3. Select the filter: "Passives in Radius of [Keystone] can be allocated without being connected."
4. Confirm the item is **Corrupted** (every copy is).
5. Confirm **Limited to: 1** (it is the unique, not a renamed rare).
6. Filter to the current Early Access league and online sellers.

## Value drivers

- Bound Keystone relevance (popular build Keystones = high price).
- Always filter by exact Keystone; do not copy an old trade link.
- Prices are volatile; treat any specific number as a snapshot for 2026-08-03.
