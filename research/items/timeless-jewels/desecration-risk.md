# Desecration Risk — Timeless Jewels (extra)

Focused analysis of Undying Hate's Unstable Desecration (plan §15.7). P0 rule: do NOT state a fixed destruction percentage.

## Mechanic
- Undying Hate can be Desecrated up to 4 times using Preserved Craniums.
- Each Desecration adds a Desecrated modifier and makes the jewel Unstable.
- The 0.3.1 patch introduced the "Unstable Desecration" keyword.

## Official wording (the only safe statement)
- Desecration makes the item Unstable and the destruction probability INCREASES with each use.
- No fixed percentage is published by GGG / PoE2Wiki / poe2db.

## Risk tiers (qualitative only)
- 0 Desecrations: no extra desecration risk.
- 1 Desecration: risk begins; probability rises with each use (official: increasing).
- 2 Desecrations: higher than at 1; still no fixed % published.
- 3-4 Desecrations: destruction becomes a real possibility; probability keeps rising.

## Recommendations
- Back up the tooltip before any crafting.
- Most builds stop at 2-3 Desecrated mods.
- Only push to 4 if the build strictly needs it; keep a backup jewel.
- Community sample estimates exist but are NOT official; this guide never states a fixed destruction percentage.

## Why no fixed number
- Stating a fixed % (e.g. "10% per use") would be unsourced and contradicts the official "increasing probability" phrasing. The published article therefore uses qualitative tiers only.
