# Passive Tree Interactions — Timeless Jewels

- A Timeless Jewel is socketed into a normal allocated Jewel Socket on the passive tree.
- Its Very Large radius (1500) conquers every small and notable passive inside it, replacing them with themed versions.
- Conquered nodes cannot be further modified by other radius jewels.
- Keystone passives inside radius are transformed by the jewel's name (leader/faction).
- Only one Historic (Timeless) jewel is allowed across the whole tree — a second is blocked.
- Seed (number) decides Notable outcomes; name decides Keystone outcomes; Divine rerolls both.
- Because radius is fixed and large, socket choice decides which nodes are conquered — a final decision for the build.
