# Current vs Legacy — Timeless Jewels

Historical issues are separated from current rules (plan §16).

## Undying Hate Tecrod Sacrifice of Blood

- Current (0.5.4): 1 Life/sec per 16 Life spent in past 4s, 20% more Life Cost of Skills (0.4.0 change).
- Legacy (pre-0.4.0): 8 Life/sec per 8 Life spent, 50% more Life Cost. INVALID as current; old 0.1/0.2 guides wrong.

## Unstable Desecration

- Current (0.5.4): present since 0.3.1; up to 4 Preserved Craniums, increasing destruction probability.
- Legacy (pre-0.3.1): no Unstable Desecration keyword.

## Base introduction

- Current: exists since 0.1.0.
- Legacy: no Timeless Jewel pre-0.1.0.

Never present a legacy value as the current 0.5.4 value. The 0.4 keystone change is the most common outdated claim to catch.
