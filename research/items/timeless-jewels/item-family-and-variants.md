# Item Family and Variants — Timeless Jewels

Members covered: Heroic Tragedy (Kalguur), Undying Hate (Abyssal), and the Timeless (Historic) family.

## Notes

- Timeless = Historic Unique jewels that conquer in-radius passives with themed versions.
- Heroic Tragedy: Kalguur theme, leader-driven Keystones, seed 100-8000, Olroth D4 drop. No desecration.
- Undying Hate: Abyssal theme, faction-driven Keystones, seed 79-30977, Tribute scaling, Vessel of Kulemak drop, desecratable up to 4.
- Both share Very Large radius and the 1-Historic limit.
- Time-Lost Jewels are a DIFFERENT base (different pool) — not a Timeless member.
- Other Unique jewels (The Adorned, From Nothing, Controlled Metamorphosis, Megalomaniac, Voices) do not conquer radius passives like Timeless.

## Variant axis

- The variants are the seed (number) and the name (leader/faction). Same name + different seed = different Notables. Same seed + different socket = different conquered set.
