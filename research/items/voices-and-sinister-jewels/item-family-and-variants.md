# Item Family and Variants — Voices and Sinister Jewel Sockets

Members covered: Voices (Sapphire Unique), Simulacrum drop context, and the Sinister Socket mechanic.

## Notes

- Basic jewels: direct mods, tree sockets, radius applies.
- Time-Lost / Against the Darkness: tree radius jewels; radius applies.
- Timeless: Expedition Seed/Keystone conquest; radius applies.
- Voices (Sinister): allocates gear-side Sinister Sockets next to portrait; radius does NOT apply.
- Sinister Sockets accept Magic/Rare jewels only; Unique jewels are excluded (hard rule confirmed by PoE2Wiki + GameRant).
- Corrupted Uniques (Voices) cannot be channelled or further crafted.
- Simulacrum is the gated source; not a tradable "drop from a boss" in the usual sense.

## Variant axis

- The only variant is socket count: 2 / 3 / 4. There is no separate "named" Voices family member.
