# Community Questions — Voices and Sinister Jewel Sockets

Three+ real questions addressed (see community-evidence section):

1. "Can I put a Unique jewel in a Sinister Socket?" -> No. Unique jewels are explicitly excluded; only Magic/Rare jewels fit (PoE2Wiki + GameRant).
2. "Does Voices give radius bonuses?" -> No. Sinister Sockets are beside the portrait, not on the tree; radius never applies (PoE2Wiki + guides).
3. "Do I spend passive points for the sockets?" -> No extra passive points; the cost is the unique slot + 1-Limited rule (PoE2Wiki + GameRant).
4. "How do I get Voices?" -> Simulacrum drop (PoE2Wiki-Simulacrum + GameRant).
5. "Is 4-socket better than 2?" -> Yes for jewel-heavy builds; more slots = more jewel scaling; 2/3 are budget steps (guides).
6. "Can I change the socket count?" -> No; the count (2/3/4) is fixed by the drop variant (PoE2Wiki).

Structure: platform/date -> player question -> current applicability -> official/database rule -> direct answer -> checks -> internal link.
