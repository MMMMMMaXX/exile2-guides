# Passive Tree Interactions — Voices and Sinister Jewel Sockets

- Voices allocates Sinister Jewel Sockets beside the character portrait, NOT on the passive tree.
- Because the sockets are gear/portrait-side, passive-tree radius rules and Jewel Socket allocation do not apply to them.
- No passive point is spent to enable the allocated Sinister Sockets (the cost is the unique item slot + the 1-Limited rule).
- Jewels slotted in Sinister Sockets still provide their own modifiers; those mods are not multiplied by tree radius.
- Unlike tree radius jewels, overlapping-radius interactions do not occur for Sinister Sockets.
- A tree Jewel Socket and a Sinister Socket are independent: you can use both simultaneously.
