# Radius and Placement — Voices and Sinister Jewel Sockets

## Radius

- Voices has NO passive-tree radius. The Sinister Sockets are placed next to the portrait automatically based on the allocated count (2/3/4).
- Radius jewels near the portrait do not interact with Sinister Sockets.

## Placement rules

- Slot Magic/Rare jewels only; Unique jewels are rejected by the socket.
- The number of sockets (2/3/4) is fixed by the item variant you own; you cannot change it.
- Place high-value scaling jewels (attributes, life, resist, crit, etc.) into the Sinister Sockets for builds that would otherwise path far for tree sockets.
- No travel cost: ideal for off-tree jewel scaling.
- Avoid wasting sockets on low-value jewels; each of the 2/3/4 slots is premium real estate.

## Value curve

- 4 sockets: maximum jewel-scaling, premium.
- 3 sockets: strong mid-high budget.
- 2 sockets: entry/budget enabling.
