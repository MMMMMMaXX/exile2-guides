# Build Usage — Pillar of the Caged God

The Pillar is for quarterstaff attackers who can out-stack Dexterity.

## Primary homes
1. **Invoker Dex-Stacking Monk** — stack Dexterity for evasion/ES (from Dex) and the Pillar multiplier. Use Hand of Wisdom and Action + Astramentis to push past 800 Dex.
2. **Quarterstaff Deadeye** — Lightning Arrow / Ice Strike variant; Dex scales the multiplier plus proj/attack speed, reaching 500-700 Dex for +1100-1540% attack damage.
3. **Gemling Legionnaire (attribute-respec)** — dump everything into Dexterity and ride the per-10-Dex scaling while meeting other gear via conversion rings.
4. **Blink / charged-staff melee** — Tempest Flurry, Falling Thunder benefit from the flat attack-damage multiplier; stack Dex for damage and accuracy.

## Hard prerequisites
- A large amount of Dexterity (ideally 500+). The staff is dead weight at ~100 Dex.
- Conversion gear so the rest of your equipment fits on pure Dexterity.

## Anti-synergy (do NOT use)
- Builds that need weapon-local flat damage and accuracy from the staff base.
- Characters that cannot reach high Dexterity.
