# Current vs Legacy — Pillar of the Caged God

> VERIFIED against PoE2Wiki (Version history + Legacy variants sections). Current as of Path of Exile 2 Early Access 0.5.x.

## Current (0.5.x, post-0.2.0)
Pillar of the Caged God is a unique **Long Quarterstaff** (Physical 9–18, Crit 10%, APS 1.40, Range 1.3m). Requires **Level 4, 9 Dexterity**. Three modifiers:

1. `10% increased Weapon Damage per 10 Strength`
2. `1% increased Attack Speed per 10 Dexterity`
3. `1% increased Area of Effect for Attacks per 10 Intelligence`

Implicit: `16% increased Melee Strike Range with this weapon`.

This is the only current version in Early Access 0.5.x. It drops anywhere and can be created with an Orb of Chance.

## Legacy / earlier EA (pre-0.2.0)
- Introduced in **0.1.0** as a Long Quarterstaff unique.
- Pre-0.2.0 modifier rates were stronger:
  - `10% increased Weapon Damage per 5 Strength` (double the current rate)
  - `2% increased Attack Speed per 10 Dexterity` (current: 1%)
  - `2% increased Area of Effect per 10 Intelligence` (current: 1%)
- **There is NO Dexterity-only "(16–22)% increased Attack Damage per 10 Dexterity" modifier, and the staff does NOT convert requirements to Dexterity.** Both claims are incorrect and must not be used.

## What changed across patches
- **0.2.0 (Dawn of the Hunt):** rebalanced the staff — now `1% increased Attack Speed per 10 Dexterity` and `1% increased Area of Effect per 10 Intelligence` (previously both 2%), and `10% increased Weapon Damage per 10 Strength` (previously per 5 Strength). The patch note states the per-attribute changes **affect existing items** automatically, while a Divine Orb can obtain the remaining value changes.
- **0.1.0:** introduced to the game.
- No separate tradeable "legacy" item exists — the older scaling was automatically converted on every owned Pillar. Verifying version is done by reading the modifier text (`per 10 Strength` = current; `per 5 Strength` = legacy/no longer obtainable).
