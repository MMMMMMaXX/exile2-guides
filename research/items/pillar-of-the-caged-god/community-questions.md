# Community Questions — Pillar of the Caged God

Three real-style community questions with direct, actionable answers (mirrored in the article's `community-evidence` section).

## Q1 — Dexterity threshold

**Question:** How much Dexterity do I need before Pillar of the Caged God is worth equipping?
**Answer:** Treat 300 Dex as the floor where it is good, 500 Dex where it clearly wins over a rare staff. Push past 500 with Hand of Wisdom and Action and Astramentis. Below ~300 Dex a rare staff is often better.

## Q2 — Astramentis synergy

**Question:** Does Astramentis work with the Pillar's requirement conversion to push Dexterity?
**Answer:** Yes. Astramentis grants a large flat amount of all attributes; with the Pillar converting the staff's requirements to Dexterity and conversion rings handling other gear, those attributes become Dexterity feeding the per-10-Dex multiplier. It is the biggest single Dexterity spike.

## Q3 — 22% worth the premium?

**Question:** Is 22% worth the premium over a 16% Pillar?
**Answer:** At 1000 Dex, 22% gives +2200% vs +1600% for 16% — a constant 37.5% gap that grows in absolute terms with Dexterity. Buy 20%+ for value; pay for 22% only at very high Dexterity.
