# Alternatives — Pillar of the Caged God

## vs Rare Quarterstaff

- A rare staff brings its own flat physical damage, attack speed, crit and accuracy plus sockets.
- **Use the rare staff when** you cannot stack Dexterity high; the Pillar underperforms below ~300 Dex.

## vs Other unique quarterstaves

- The Pillar has no elemental conversion or ailment mechanic of its own — only the Dexterity scaling.
- Uniques with flat damage, penetration or triggered skills are simpler, self-contained.
- **Use those when** you want a self-contained weapon, not a Dex-stacking engine.

## vs Mirror-tier rare staff (endgame)

- At the very top end a mirror-tier rare staff can exceed the Pillar, but costs far more currency.
- The Pillar is the Dex-stacking choice for quarterstaves; no direct competitor.

## Decision rule

- Invest in Dex-stacking + Pillar = cheap per point of attack damage early/mid.
- Invest in a self-contained rare/unique staff = simpler, stronger late but more expensive.
