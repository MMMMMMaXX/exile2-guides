# Megalomaniac — Radius and Placement

## Radius Is Irrelevant

Megalomaniac allocates Notables directly via its implicit; it does **not** grant jewel-socket passives and does **not** depend on a radius. You can socket it in **any** available jewel socket on the passive tree.

## Placement Guidance

1. Open the Passive Tree and find **any free Jewel Socket** you can reach (or already pass through).
2. Socket Megalomaniac there.
3. The 2–3 allocated Notables are granted immediately — no radius coverage needed.

## Optimization Tip

Because placement is flexible, prefer a socket that you are already pathing through (zero opportunity cost). Unlike The Adorned, there is no benefit to clustering sockets around it.

## Sinister Sockets

Megalomaniac is not an Expression/Simulacrum-socket jewel and is not placed in a Sinister Socket.
