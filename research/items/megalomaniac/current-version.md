# Megalomaniac — Current Version (0.5.4)

Verified current mechanics for Path of Exile 2 Early Access 0.5.4.

## Item Line

- **Base:** Diamond
- **Rarity:** Unique
- **Item Level:** 20 (can be used from level 20)
- **Limited to:** 1
- **Corrupted:** Yes — always corrupted on drop
- **Drop state:** Identified (since patch 0.5.0)

## Core Modifier

> **Allocates (2–3) Random Notable Passive Skills**
> **Limited to 1**

The implicit roll determines whether the jewel grants **2** (lower roll) or **3** (higher roll) random Notables. Because it drops identified, you see the exact allocated Notables before equipping or buying.

## Drop Source

Megalomaniac drops in **Simulacrum** encounters, specifically from the Delirium bosses that appear in later waves:

- **Omniphobia, the Living Mirage**
- **The Fear Manifest**
- **Kosis the Archivist**

Higher Simulacrum waves (and the bosses) are the reliable source.

## Usability Constraints

- Cannot be modified by Divine Orb, Vaal Orb, or Architect's Orb (it is corrupted).
- Notables allocated are random — you cannot choose them.
- The allocated Notables still obey normal tree rules (you must be able to reach/own them conceptually; they are granted for free regardless of pathing).

## Why It Is Strong

Each allocated Notable is normally worth 1–3 passive points. A 3-Notable Megalomaniac can save up to ~9 passive points (or grant powerful Notables you could not otherwise reach), making it one of the highest-value unique jewels for flexible tree building.
