# Megalomaniac — Community Questions

Real questions aggregated from r/PathOfExile2 and video comments, with this site's analysis.

## Q1: "Can I choose which Notables it gives?"

**Reported:** Player expected to pick Notables.

**Analysis:** No — the allocated Notables are **random** per drop. You can only filter trade for a copy that already rolled the ones you want. — `poe2db-megalomaniac`, `reddit-megalomaniac`.

## Q2: "Does it drop identified now?"

**Reported:** Confusion vs old guides saying "buy unIDs."

**Analysis:** Since **0.5.0** it drops **identified**. Old "bulk unID flip" advice is outdated (see `current-vs-legacy.md`). — `official-05-4`, `gamerant-megalomaniac`.

## Q3: "Can I Divine/Adorn it to change the Notables?"

**Reported:** Player wanted to reroll Notables.

**Analysis:** Impossible — it is **Corrupted on drop**, so Divine/Vaal/Architect's Orb cannot modify it. — `poe2wiki-megalomaniac`.

## Q4: "Where do I farm it?"

**Reported:** New player unsure of source.

**Analysis:** Drops in **Simulacrum** from Delirium bosses (Omniphobia, The Fear Manifest, Kosis). Also buyable on trade. — `poe2wiki-megalomaniac`, `gamerant-megalomaniac`.

## Q5: "Is 2 or 3 Notables better, and how is it decided?"

**Reported:** Player asked what roll gives 3.

**Analysis:** The implicit roll decides: higher roll → **3** Notables, lower roll → **2**. Always prefer a 3-Notable roll for value. — `poe2db-megalomaniac`, `timesaver-megalomaniac`.

## Q6: "Do the Notables need pathing?"

**Reported:** Player worried about reachability.

**Analysis:** No — they are granted free regardless of your tree pathing. — `poe2wiki-megalomaniac`.
