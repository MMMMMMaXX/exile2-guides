# Megalomaniac — Current vs Legacy

## Key Change: Identified on Drop (0.5.0)

| Aspect              | Legacy (pre-0.5.0)              | Current (0.5.4)                                                  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Drop identification | Dropped **unidentified**        | Drops **identified**                                             |
| Buyer visibility    | Had to identify to see Notables | Notables visible immediately                                     |
| Trade filtering     | Could not pre-filter by Notable | Can filter by allocated Notable (see `trade-filter-examples.md`) |

Before 0.5.0, a Megalomaniac dropped unidentified, so you could not see which Notables it granted until you identified it (consuming an identify currency). Since 0.5.0 it drops already identified, which transformed both farming strategy and trade search.

## Unchanged Across Versions

- Base Diamond, Unique, Item Level 20.
- Limited to 1.
- Corrupted on drop.
- Drops in Simulacrum from Delirium bosses.
- Allocates 2–3 random Notables (the 2/3 count on roll has been consistent; theIdentification change is the headline difference).

## Practical Takeaway

- **Legacy advice** telling players to "bulk buy unIDs and chaose them" is **outdated** — current drops are identified, so trade filtering by Notable is the modern approach.
- Do **not** copy old "unID flip" strategies into current builds.
