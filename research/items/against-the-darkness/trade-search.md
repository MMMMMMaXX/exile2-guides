# Trade Search — Against the Darkness

## Steps (current UI)

1. Item Category: Jewel
2. Rarity: Unique (or Normal/Magic/Rare for basic)
3. Name: Against the Darkness (or base for others)
4. Read each listing's modifiers — the official site cannot filter both Against the Darkness modifiers at once.
5. Confirm the exact Socket and Radius for your tree.
6. League: current; filter Online/Offline as needed.

Do not hardcode long-term Trade URLs in static copy.
