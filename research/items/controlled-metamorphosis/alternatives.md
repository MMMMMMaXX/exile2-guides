# Alternatives — Controlled Metamorphosis

Article ID: controlled-metamorphosis

## Direct alternatives

- **Rare Diamond Jewel**: flat stats, no resistance cost, no ring-size gamble. Usually safer when target nodes are cheap to path to.
- **From Nothing**: fixed disc remote allocation, no resistance cost, but Keystone-locked.
- **Megalomaniac**: direct random Notable allocation.
- **Against the Darkness**: boosts small passives in a radius.
- **Normal pathing**: often cheaper than a wrong-ring-size copy.

## When NOT to use

- Your target nodes sit in the dead inner circle or outside the band at the rolled size.
- You cannot compensate the Elemental Resistance on suffixes.
- The socket is better used for a Rare/Unique jewel without a resistance cost.
