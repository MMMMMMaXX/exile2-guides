# Radius and Placement — Controlled Metamorphosis

Article ID: controlled-metamorphosis

## Radius facts
- Shape: **annulus (ring band)**, not a filled disc. This is the defining difference from From Nothing.
- Size rolls 1-8: Very Small, Small, Medium-Small, Medium, Medium-Large, Large, Large-Very Large, Massive.
- Only the band between the two rings allocates; the inner circle and everything outside the outer ring are excluded.
- Ring size is independent of the Elemental Resistance roll.

## Placement strategy
- Choose a socket you can reach cheaply; the useful nodes must sit on the band at the rolled size.
- A Massive ring is most valuable when target nodes land on its band AND your gear can absorb the Elemental penalty.
- Trade value rises with ring size regardless of fit, so buy for your tree, not for size alone.

## Comparison to From Nothing
- Controlled Metamorphosis: annulus, Elemental Resistance cost, ring-size 1-8.
- From Nothing: filled disc, no resistance cost, fixed Keystone-bound radius.
