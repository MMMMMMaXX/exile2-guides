# Passive Tree Interactions — Controlled Metamorphosis

Article ID: controlled-metamorphosis

## How the tree responds

1. Socket the jewel in an **already allocated** Jewel Socket.
2. Two concentric rings form an annulus; only the band between them allocates nodes.
3. The inner circle is dead space — nodes there cannot be taken via the jewel.
4. Nodes beyond the outer ring must be pathed normally.
5. You still pay the passive point for every allocated node; only pathing is skipped.
6. Removing the jewel unallocates all ring nodes and refunds their points.

## Ring-size verbal diagram (original passive-tree diagram in prose)

- Place the Jewel Socket at the centre of a grid of nodes.
- Draw inner circle radius r1 and outer circle radius r2.
- The annulus = the donut band between r1 and r2. Nodes on the donut are reachable; nodes strictly inside r1 and outside r2 are not.
- Because the band is thin, the 1-8 roll decides how many useful nodes land on it — larger is not automatically better.

## Resistance interaction

- -(20-5)% all Elemental Resistances must be recovered on gear suffixes.
- Chaos Inoculation builds ignore the (former) Chaos penalty and soften the Elemental cost.
