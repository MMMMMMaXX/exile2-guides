# Item Family and Variants — Controlled Metamorphosis

Article ID: controlled-metamorphosis

## Diamond Unique Jewel family

Member of the five build-enabling Diamond Unique Jewels (see family-overview section):

- Against the Darkness (radius attribute)
- From Nothing (fixed disc remote allocation, no resistance cost)
- Controlled Metamorphosis (ring-band remote allocation, Elemental cost) — this article
- The Adorned (Magic jewel amp)
- Megalomaniac (random Notables)

## Variants of Controlled Metamorphosis itself

The only variant axis is the **ring size roll (1-8)** plus the **Elemental Resistance roll (-(20-5)%)**. There are no named sub-items; trade value tracks ring size first, then resistance. A copy with ring value 9+ is a bug-affected outlier (documented), not a variant.
