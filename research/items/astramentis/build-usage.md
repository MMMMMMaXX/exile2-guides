# Build Usage - Astramentis (星芒之链)

- **Hand of Wisdom and Action Dex/Int stacker**: signature home; +50/60 fills the (Dex+Int) pool and eases gem reqs.
- **Pillar of the Caged God Strength stacker**: +Strength toward the staff scaling pool.
- **Attribute-flex leveling**: level-1 amulet meets awkward gem/gear reqs without dumping passives.
- **Hybrid caster needing Int**: covers one high-Int gem cheaply.

Hard prerequisite: an attribute bottleneck or a stacking unique. Without it, a rare amulet is stronger.
