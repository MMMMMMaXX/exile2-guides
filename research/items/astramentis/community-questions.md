# Community Questions - Astramentis (星芒之链)

1. **Q: Is Astramentis the fix for a Dex/Int stacking weapon with low attributes?**
   A: Yes - HoWA scales with (Dex+Int); a +50 roll adds 100 to that pool and eases gem reqs. Use 55-60.

2. **Q: For Pillar of the Caged God, does Astramentis help or stack pure Strength?**
   A: The +Strength half helps; Dex/Int half covers other reqs. A +Strength rare amulet may out-scale if Strength is your only gate.

3. **Q: Still good after the 0.2 attribute rework?**
   A: Yes - rework made attributes more offensive, so a flat floor is more valuable. Buy 55-60.

4. **Q: What roll and corruption behaviour?**
   A: Buy 55-60 for value; corruption adds an implicit, not a higher all-attributes number.
