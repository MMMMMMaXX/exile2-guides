# Alternatives - Astramentis (星芒之链)

- **Rare amber amulet**: better when you already meet requirements / use few charms; brings life, resists, attributes and an open suffix.
- **Other unique amulets**: stronger when you want a specific mechanic rather than a flat floor / charm multiplier.
- **No direct competitor**: Astramentis is the only level-1 all-attributes amulet.

Decision: flat floor is cheaper early; tailored rare wins late but costs more to craft.
