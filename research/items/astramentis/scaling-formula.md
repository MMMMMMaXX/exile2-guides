# Scaling Formula - Astramentis (星芒之链)

A single +(40-60) line adds the same number N to Strength, Dexterity and Intelligence at once. Requirement-gating example: a 111-Int gem with a 100-Int character is covered by a +60 roll. Attribute-stacking: Hand of Wisdom and Action gains (Dex+Int) pool points; Pillar of the Caged God gains Strength pool points.

## Worked example
- Roll 50 -> +50 Str, +50 Dex, +50 Int.
- Hand of Wisdom and Action (Dex+Int weapon): +50 contributes 100 points to its scaling pool.
- Pillar of the Caged God (Strength staff): +50 (or +60 at max roll) adds to its Strength pool.
- Gem gate: 100 Int character + 60 Astramentis = 160 Int, covering a 111-Int gem with margin.

## Caveats
- Per-point attribute conversions (PoE2 game constants) should be confirmed in-game.
- Ingenuity radius only matters for charms with an area effect.
