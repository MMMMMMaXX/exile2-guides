# Widowhail — Community Questions (answered with sources)

All answers below are reproduced in the article's `community-evidence` section and link to a source-ledger entry.

## Q1 — Spark caster + a bow? (forum-widowhail-spark)
- Source: Official forum Spark Infernalist build thread (source-ledger #6).
- Question: How does a bow (Widowhail) work on a spell caster, and which quiver stats matter?
- Direct answer: Widowhail is used purely for its quiver multiplier. Take a quiver with +2 to projectile skill levels and very high projectile speed; the multiplied projectile scaling feeds Projectile Acceleration and Spark's per-hit damage. The bow never attacks.
- Practical floor: 230%+ Widowhail.

## Q2 — Poisonous Concoction quiver affixes (forum-widowhail-pconc)
- Source: Community forum PC setup thread (source-ledger #7).
- Question: Which quiver mods to maximize on Widowhail for PC?
- Direct answer: Stack +2 Projectile Skill Levels (most important), then attack speed (≥14%) and accuracy on the quiver. Widowhail multiplies all three; Master Fletching adds further multipliers.

## Q3 — Good for leveling or only endgame? (maxroll-leveling-uniques)
- Source: MaxRoll Best Leveling Uniques (source-ledger #5).
- Question: Is Widowhail actually good during the campaign?
- Direct answer: Yes — from ~level 35 a well-quivered Widowhail beats Rare leveling bows of equal cost; scaling life-gain-on-hit, skill speed and gem levels makes it a strong campaign bow.

## Q4 — What roll to buy / can it exceed 250%? (gamerant-widowhail)
- Source: GameRant How to Get Widowhail (source-ledger #4).
- Question: Which roll should I buy, and can corruption exceed the natural cap?
- Direct answer: Buy 240%+ for best value; only pay the premium for a natural 250% if budget allows. Corruption can exceed 250% (community ~290%+) but may add an unwanted implicit — corrupt a replaceable copy.

## Recurring theme
- "The quiver is the engine; the bow is the amplifier." Every answered question reduces to: invest in the quiver first, tailor it to YOUR skill's damage tags, then buy the highest affordable roll.
