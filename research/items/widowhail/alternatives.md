# Widowhail — Alternatives Research

## Thesis
Widowhail is NOT a "more DPS no matter what" bow. Its value is conditional on the quiver. If you cannot source a quiver worth amplifying, a well-rolled Rare bow outperforms it.

## Option A — Rare bow
- Brings its own flat physical damage, attack speed, critical modifiers and sockets.
- Better when you lack a strong quiver.
- Decision factor: with a weak quiver, a Rare bow's self-contained damage wins.

## Option B — Other unique bows
- Bows that add flat damage, penetration or ailment conversion have simpler, self-contained mechanics and no quiver dependency.
- Better when you want a self-contained weapon rather than a quiver engine.

## Option C — Widowhail + quiver (the actual choice)
- Within the quiver-scaling fantasy there is NO direct competitor — Widowhail is the only bow that scales the equipped quiver.
- Real decision: "invest in quiver + Widowhail" vs "invest same currency in a stronger self-contained Rare or unique bow".
- Early/mid campaign: quiver route is usually cheaper per point of power.
- Very top end: a mirror-tier Rare bow can exceed it but costs far more.

## Risk of wrong alternative
- Picking Widowhail without a quiver plan = dead weight (the roll alone creates no power).
- Picking a Rare bow while sitting on a great quiver = leaving damage on the table.
