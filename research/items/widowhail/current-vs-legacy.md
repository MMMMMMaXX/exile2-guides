# Widowhail — Current vs Legacy

## Current natural-drop version (0.5.4)
- Base: Crude Bow (Unique).
- Required Level: 1 (14 Dexterity).
- Single unique modifier: (150–250)% increased bonuses gained from Equipped Quiver.
- Roll tiers: 150% / 200% / 250% (snaps to 50% steps; no 187% roll).
- Base physical damage 5–13, 1.40 attacks/sec, 5.00% crit (inherited from Crude Bow base).
- No separate legacy quiver-bonus value to confuse with the current one — the (150–250)% line is the only current version of the quiver-bonus mechanic.

## Runeforging (0.5.0 league — Runes of Aldur)
- Unique Runeforging introduced, letting low-level uniques (requirement below 55) be upgraded.
- Widowhail (L1) qualifies: the unique quiver-bonus mod is retained; the base gains higher physical damage plus a Runeforged (Runic Ward) base.
- A Runeforged Widowhail is a distinct base type, listed separately from the normal unique on trade.

## Divine Orb / corruption behaviour
- The quiver-bonus roll is a fixed range, so a Divine Orb does NOT change it (no reroll within tiers).
- A Vaal Orb corruption CAN exceed 250% (community reports up to ~290%+) but may add an unwanted implicit.
- Neither Divine nor corruption converts a natural-drop version into the Runeforged version; Runeforging is a separate, deliberate craft.

## How Trade identifies the version
- Trade site shows the exact "increased bonuses gained from Equipped Quiver" percentage; buyers filter on it directly.
- Runeforged Widowhail = distinct Runic Ward base, separate listing from the normal unique.

## Note on "legacy" wording
- Unlike some uniques, Widowhail has no competing older quiver-bonus percentage from a prior patch that needs a side-by-side. The only historical nuances are the Runeforging addition (0.5.0) and the corruption-over-250% possibility. Documented as current vs Runeforged vs corruption rather than a percentage legacy.
