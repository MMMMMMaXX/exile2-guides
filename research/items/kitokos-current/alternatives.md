# Alternatives — Kitoko's Current

Checked date: 2026-08-03
Verification status: pending-pc

Alternatives considered for the "Lightning-hit Electrocution / CC glove" slot. The goal is
to be transparent about what else a player could use instead, and why Kitoko's Current is
or is not the pick.

## 1. Other unique gloves (general)

- **High-ES / Dex unique gloves**: provide defense or attribute value but lack the
  Electrocution-buildup signature. Pick them if you need raw stats over CC.
- **Attack-speed unique gloves**: offset the speed tax but give no Electrocution. Pick if
  speed is your bottleneck and you do not need CC.

## 2. Reach the same outcome another way

- **Shock-focused gear**: if your goal is "enemy takes more damage" rather than "enemy is
  stunned," Shock-enabling uniques beat Electrocution for pure damage amplification.
- **Direct stun/CC gems or threshold jewels**: can apply control without the glove, but
  usually cost a gem/support slot and do not convert your Lightning damage into buildup.
- **Charms / ailment-threshold guides**: the ailments-thresholds-charms-immunity guide
  explains threshold tuning that complements or replaces the glove's approach.

## 3. Crafted / rare gloves

- A well-rolled rare Jewelled Gloves can exceed the glove's ES and add speed, but cannot
  reproduce the signature Electrocution mod. The unique is the only source of that effect.

## 4. Decision summary

| If you want…                     | Pick                        |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Lightning hits that also stun/CC | Kitoko's Current            |
| Pure damage amplification        | Shock-enabling unique       |
| Max ES / attributes              | High-stat unique or rare    |
| No speed tax                     | Attack-speed unique or rare |

## Uncertain / flagged

- Specific competing unique names are not hard-asserted with prices; the article links the
  ailments guide and related builds instead of naming every alternative. Keeps the piece
  from drifting into unverified comparisons.
