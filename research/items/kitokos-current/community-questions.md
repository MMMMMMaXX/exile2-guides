# Community Questions — Kitoko's Current

Checked date: 2026-08-03
Verification status: pending-pc

Questions synthesized from r/PathOfExile2 discussion and the official forum build thread
(ru.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/26105444). These reflect what players actually ask
and are answered (or flagged) in the article.

## 1. Does the Electrocution mod work with Damage over Time?

No. The signature mod specifies **Lightning damage from Hits**. DoT does not contribute to
Electrocution buildup via this glove. Confirmed by two explain sources.

## 2. Is the speed penalty "reduced" or "less"?

It is **reduced** (additive with other reduced modifiers before the final multiply), which
is easier to offset than "less" (multiplicative). The article's Speed Penalty table shows
the 10-15% range.

## 3. Electrocution vs Shock — which one does this glove give?

Electrocution (a stun/control ailment). Shock is a separate ailment that increases damage
the enemy takes. The glove builds Electrocution, not Shock. The Electrocution vs Shock
table in the article separates the two.

## 4. Does Electrocution threshold scale with enemy life?

Yes, per the gathered mechanic explanations, the Electrocution threshold scales with the
target's maximum life, so higher-life enemies need more buildup. (Exact coefficient not
published; pending-pc.)

## 5. Can I get it from a divination card or only random drop?

Acquisition per database: world-drop unique; can also be created via Orbs of Chance from a
Jewelled Gloves base (chance outcome). No dedicated divination card asserted.

## 6. Is it worth the speed loss on a cast build?

Qualitatively yes for CC-focused Lightning-cast builds; offset the penalty with increased
cast speed elsewhere. No single measured DPS number is asserted; build-specific.
