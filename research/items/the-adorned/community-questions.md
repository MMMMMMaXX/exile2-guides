# The Adorned — Community Questions

Real questions aggregated from r/PathOfExile2 and video comments, with this site's analysis.

## Q1: "Why isn't my magic jewel getting amplified?"

**Reported:** Player socketed a normal/rare jewel near The Adorned and saw no change.

**Analysis:** The Adorned only amplifies **Corrupted Magic Jewels** (magic rarity + corrupted). A rare jewel or a non-corrupted magic jewel is ignored. The fix is to corrupt a magic jewel with a Vaal Orb (see `magic-jewel-crafting.md`). — `reddit-adorned`, `youtube-adorned`.

## Q2: "Can I Divine/improve the roll?"

**Reported:** Player asked if a low roll could be raised.

**Analysis:** The Adorned is **Corrupted on drop**, so Divine Orbs, Vaal Orbs, and Architect's Orbs cannot modify it. You must trade for a higher roll or farm another drop. — `poe2wiki-adorned`.

## Q3: "Does it work on unique jewels like Astramentis?"

**Reported:** Confusion about whether any jewel in radius is boosted.

**Analysis:** Only Corrupted Magic Jewels qualify. Unique jewels (Astramentis, etc.) are explicitly excluded. — `poe2db-adorned`.

## Q4: "My old breakpoint table says 100% max — is that wrong?"

**Reported:** Player used a legacy 0–100% calculator.

**Analysis:** Correct concern — pre-0.4.0 the cap was 100%. Current cap is **150%**. Using old tables undervalues the jewel. See `current-vs-legacy.md` and `effect-breakpoints.csv`. — `official-05-4`.

## Q5: "Is the radius Small or can it be bigger?"

**Reported:** Player expected a large radius.

**Analysis:** The Adorned uses the standard **Small** radius; there is no larger-radius variant documented. Placement near clustered sockets is the lever, not radius size. — `poe2wiki-adorned`.

## Q6: "Where do I actually get one?"

**Reported:** New player unsure of source.

**Analysis:** Drops from **The Trialmaster** (Trial of Chaos capstone). Also buyable on trade. — `gamerant-adorned`, `poe2wiki-adorned`.
