# Current Version — The Adorned (0.5.4)

Verified against poe2wiki and poe2db on 2026-08-03.

## Identity

- Type: Unique Diamond Jewel
- Item class: Jewel
- Required level: 20
- Rarity: Unique
- Limited to: 1
- Corrupted on drop (roll locked)

## Modifier (current 0.5.4)

> (0-150)% increased Effect of Jewel Socket Passive Skills containing Corrupted Magic Jewels

The roll is random between 0% and 150%. A perfect 150% roll multiplies each qualifying jewel's effect by 2.5.

## What it affects

- Only Corrupted Magic Jewels (blue, two-modifier jewels that have been corrupted, usually with a Vaal Orb).
- Only jewels socketed in the passive skill tree inside the Adorned's radius.
- The effect scales the jewel socket's passive skills (the magic jewel's explicit modifiers).

## What it does NOT affect

- Rare Jewels (3-4 modifiers)
- Unique Jewels (including The Adorned itself)
- Uncorrupted Magic Jewels
- Jewels socketed into equipment (e.g. Sekhema's Resolve)
- Jewels in Sinister Jewel Sockets
- Time-Lost jewels used as the radius anchor

## Acquisition

- Drops from The Trialmaster, capstone boss of the Trial of Chaos (10-room Inscribed Ultimatum at area level 75+).
- Not Chance Orb compatible.
- Can be bought on the trade site.

## Recent changes

- 0.4.0: range raised from 0-100% to 0-150%.
- 0.4.0b: Vaal Cultivation Orb can add a side modifier (e.g. -30-30% to a damage type, or 0-75% effect for Corrupted Rare Jewels).
