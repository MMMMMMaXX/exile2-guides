# The Adorned — Trade Search

## Where to Buy

The Adorned is tradeable. Because it is **always Corrupted on drop**, every listed copy already has a fixed roll in the (0–150)% range. You are shopping for the **roll value**, not for corruption potential.

## Trade Site Workflow (current UI)

1. Open the Path of Exile 2 trade site and select the **Items → Jewels** category.
2. Filter base type to **Diamond** and rarity **Unique**.
3. Search the item name **"The Adorned"**.
4. In the stat filter, add the modifier:
   `increased Effect of Jewel Socket Passive Skills that are Corrupted Magic Jewels`
5. Set a **minimum value** for that stat to skip low rolls (e.g. ≥ 120 for high-budget, ≥ 90 for budget).
6. Sort by price ascending within your roll threshold.
7. Because the item is corrupted, ignore "Corrupted" toggles — every copy is corrupted.

> Note: Do **not** hardcode a long-lived trade URL in the article. The trade UI and stat indices change between leagues; instruct readers to apply the stat filter live. (Per content-plan §13.5 guidance, no hardcoded long-term trade URL.)

## Pricing Signals

- Rolls near 150% command a steep premium (high-budget amplifier builds).
- Rolls 90–120% are the common "good enough" tier.
- Rolls below 75% are often cheaper than the build's supporting Corrupted Magic Jewels cost — frequently not worth the slot.

## Self-Sourcing Alternative

The Adorned drops from **The Trialmaster**, the capstone boss of the Trial of Chaos (fourth floor / "The Traitor" reward). Farming the trial is an alternative to buying, but the drop is not guaranteed and the roll is random.
