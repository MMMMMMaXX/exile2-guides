# The Adorned — Competitor Conflicts

Cross-checking claims against other unique jewels and guides to resolve contradictions.

## Conflict 1: Drop Boss

- **Some older guides / player memory:** associate The Adorned with generic "Trial of Chaos" or even with Simulacrum (confused with Megalomaniac).
- **Current verified:** The Adorned drops from **The Trialmaster**, the Trial of Chaos capstone. Megalomaniac drops in Simulacrum.
- **Resolution:** Keep the two drop sources distinct. The Adorned ≠ Simulacrum drop. Source: `poe2wiki-adorned`, `gamerant-adorned`.

## Conflict 2: Roll Range

- **Legacy calculators / old reddit posts:** state (0–100)%.
- **Current:** (0–150)% since 0.4.0.
- **Resolution:** Use 0–150% everywhere; flag legacy content as outdated. Source: `official-05-4`, `poe2db-adorned`.

## Conflict 3: Amplifies "any jewel"

- **Misleading short guides:** imply all jewels in radius are boosted.
- **Current:** only Corrupted Magic Jewels.
- **Resolution:** State the eligibility rule explicitly in `eligibility` and `passive-tree-interactions`. Source: `poe2db-adorned`.

## Conflict 4: Divine Orb usability

- **Occasional claim:** "roll it higher with divines."
- **Resolution:** Impossible — Corrupted on drop. Source: `poe2wiki-adorned`.
