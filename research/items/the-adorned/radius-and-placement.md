# The Adorned — Radius and Placement

## Default Radius

Like most standard jewels in Path of Exile 2, The Adorned uses the **Small** radius by default. There is no documented radius-rolling variant for this unique in 0.5.4 — the item does not carry a "Radius: Large" or similar line. Its radius is fixed at the small default.

## Why Radius Matters

The radius determines **which jewel sockets can host amplified Corrupted Magic Jewels**. Only sockets that fall inside the small circle around The Adorned's own socket are eligible. A larger available cluster of jewel sockets near the Adorned's placement multiplies its value.

## Placement Strategy

1. Open the Passive Tree and locate a region with **2 or more jewel sockets clustered together** (especially near attributes or defensive wheels you already path through).
2. Socket The Adorned in the **central** socket of that cluster so its small radius covers the neighbors.
3. Socket **Corrupted Magic Jewels** into the covered neighbor sockets.
4. Verify in the tooltip preview that the neighbor jewels' granted passive magnitudes increased.

## Avoiding Wasted Sockets

- Do not socket The Adorned next to a single isolated jewel socket — you lose most of its potential.
- Do not place it far from your path; each point spent just to reach an empty socket region is opportunity cost (see `risk-analysis.md`).
- If your build cannot reach a multi-socket region, The Adorned's value collapses and a different unique jewel may be better (see `alternatives.md`).
