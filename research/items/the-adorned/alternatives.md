# The Adorned — Alternatives

## If You Cannot Afford or Fit The Adorned

| Alternative | Type | Why | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rare Jewel (with strong explicit passives) | Rare | Cheap, socketable anywhere | Not amplified; needs good rolls |
| Against the Darkness | Unique Diamond | Conditional jewel-socket mods | Different mechanic, not a multiplier |
| Megalomaniac | Unique Diamond | Allocates 2–3 notables | Does not amplify magic jewels |
| Voices / Sinister Jewels | Unique | Special sockets | Unrelated use case |
| Attribute-stacking via gear | Gear | Avoids jewel dependency | Uses currency on gear instead |

## Comparison Summary

- **vs Rare Jewel:** A rare jewel gives fixed passives with no amplification. The Adorned multiplies *corrupted magic* jewels instead. They are not direct substitutes — The Adorned enables a strategy, a rare jewel is a filler.
- **vs Against the Darkness:** Against the Darkness modifies which jewel-socket mods are allowed/boosted under conditions; The Adorned is a flat multiplier on corrupted magic jewels.
- **vs Megalomaniac:** Megalomaniac allocates notables (free passive points). The Adorned amplifies corrupted magic jewel passives. Both are Diamond uniques, both Limited to 1, but they solve different problems and can coexist in different sockets.

## Decision

Use The Adorned when you have the socket cluster + budget. Otherwise a well-rolled Rare Jewel or a build-appropriate Unique is the pragmatic choice. See `valuation.md` for trade decision thresholds.
