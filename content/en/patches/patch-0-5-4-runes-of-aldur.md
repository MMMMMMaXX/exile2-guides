# Path of Exile 2 0.5.4 — Runes of Aldur Changes

> Verification status: Steam PC verification is pending. This is an unofficial editorial overview, not an official patch note or a gameplay recommendation.

## Patch scope

The current editorial baseline is **Path of Exile 2 Early Access 0.5.4**. The recorded client verification version is **0.5.4d**; its official notes list migration changes only, so this page does not attribute Runes of Aldur, Liquid Verisium, Atziri, or Orb of Sacrifice mechanics to that hotfix.

The official 0.5.4 preview identifies four areas for this page to examine: a Runes of Aldur Atlas Passive Tree, expanded Expedition customisation, level-scaling Unique Items, and a new currency item connected to Atziri's Temple. Those areas define the research scope; they do not yet prove a route, a build choice, an item value, or a boss strategy.

## What this page can state

- 0.5.4 is the Patch baseline for the first content batch.
- Expedition and Runes of Aldur systems need a current-Patch review before they are explained as a player workflow.
- Items and skill-granting Uniques require in-game checks for names, requirements, behaviour, and restrictions.
- Atziri-related currency and endgame encounter information must be checked in the PC client before any acquisition or strategy claim is written.

## Work that remains under review

This page deliberately does not state unverified numerical values, node effects, encounter phases, recommended skills, or build performance. The assigned fact reviewer must compare the original 0.5.4 and 0.5.4d announcements, then reproduce the relevant interactions on Steam PC before those details can be marked as verified.

If the Hotfix changes a mechanic or fixes an interaction used by a future Guide, Item, Skill, Build, or Boss article, that article must remain in `draft` or return to `under-review` until it has been retested.

## Planned follow-up articles

The current content cluster starts with the Expedition Atlas and Liquid Verisium research topics. It can later connect to verified Item, Skill, Build, and Boss articles as those pages become available.
